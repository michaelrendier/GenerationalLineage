# 8. What's next

- **A curses console.** The engine is getting a console GUI, built to merge with the monad's window and the Derivation Engine's
  curses GUI rather than as a third, separate interface. The design discussion follows this release.
- **Further moves.** The catalogue of candidate factor moves (about a hundred, tagged by where each already lives) is the queue; the
  next candidates are the Mellin/Fourier pair generalised to a multiplicative-versus-additive transform pair, Krohn–Rhodes
  decomposition of processes, and the Chebyshev/Dickson composition semigroup. Contributions: see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
